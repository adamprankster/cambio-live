The short version

Finish with the lowest score. Each player starts with four face-down cards in a 2 × 2 grid.

  1. Remember. Peek at your bottom two cards while getting ready and until your first turn begins. After that, they're locked.
  2. Draw. On your turn, take a card from the deck. Replace one of your cards, or discard the drawn card.
  3. Use a power. Discarding your drawn card activates its power. Replacing one of your cards does not activate a power.
  4. Slam. At any time after setup, tap one of your cards, then tap the discard pile to match its rank—even right after discarding your drawn card. A correct match removes your card. A mistake keeps it and adds one hidden penalty card.
  5. Call Cambio. At the start of your turn, before drawing, call Cambio instead of drawing. Every other player gets exactly one final turn, then scores are added to the running total.

7 / 8 Peek at one of your cards.

9 / 10 Peek at an opponent's card.

J / Q Blind swap one of yours with an opponent's.

Black K Peek at an opponent's card, then choose whether to swap it with one of yours.

Red K Worth −1 point; no power.

Points: A = 1; 2–10 = face value; J, Q and black K = 10; red K = −1; Joker = 0. A 54-card deck includes two jokers. The caller gets a bonus for being strictly lowest, or a penalty for a wrong or tied call (default −5 / +10; host can change this). When a player reaches the score limit (default 50), the lowest cumulative score wins. Overall ties share the win.

Empty spaces stay empty. Penalty cards can be swapped or slammed. An empty hand scores zero; the round still ends through Cambio. Powers may be skipped. Used cards refill the deck when needed, keeping the top discard.
